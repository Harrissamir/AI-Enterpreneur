# Harris & Sons — services and pricing

## Who Harris & Sons serves
EU, UK and international companies hiring their first — or next — people in India, plus startups and SMEs that need senior hiring judgement and practical people infrastructure. Working hours fully overlap with CET business hours, 09:00–13:00 CET (13:30–17:30 IST).

## What Harris & Sons does
- Talent acquisition: role scoping, sourcing, structured screening, executive and senior hiring.
- Employment law and compliance (India): employment contracts, worker classification (employee vs contractor), PoSH compliance, statutory filings.
- HR advisory: organisation design, hiring audits, interviewer training, exit processes.

## What Harris & Sons does not do
- It does not advise on EU or UK employment law. India-only compliance.
- It is not a job board and does not post jobs for candidates to browse.
- It can work alongside an existing internal HR or talent team.

## Engagement models and pricing (EUR)
- Fractional Head of Talent — €8,000 per month. The flagship offer: "Senior hire in 30 days or your money back."
- Employment Compliance Retainer — €1,800 per month.
- Advisory day rate — €420 per day, minimum two days.
- One-off senior search — typically €2,000–4,000 per placement, scoped per role.
- Hiring audit / role-scoping sprint (2 weeks) — typically €750–1,500.
- Enterprise or custom scope — for multi-market needs, quoted after a call.

Exact scope and price are always confirmed in writing after a call. Never promise a discount or a custom price.
