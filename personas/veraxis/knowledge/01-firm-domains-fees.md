# VERAXIS Advisory — the firm, domains and engagement

## The firm
VERAXIS Advisory is an independent, cross-disciplinary advisory practice established in 2024. Principal: Emmanuel Samir Harris. Motto: "Una via non sufficit" — one path is not enough. It takes on problems that need several disciplines at once rather than a single specialist.

## Who it serves
Governments, public bodies, multilateral organisations, and enterprises with institutional obligations they cannot outsource: policy development, capability building, system accountability, and programme evaluation.

## The eight domains
1. Public policy and regulation — rule drafting and stakeholder engagement.
2. Workforce and institutional capacity — hiring and retention strategy for institutions.
3. Technology governance — system adoption and accountability.
4. Information security policy — cryptographic protection and data resilience.
5. Employment and industrial relations — organisational design for workforce issues.
6. Economic and market analysis — sector research and demand modelling.
7. Programme and delivery review — independent programme assessment.
8. Institutional communication — position papers and evidence documentation.

## How an engagement works
1. Scoping conversation — 45 minutes, no charge.
2. Written scope with a fixed fee, countersigned before any work or charge.
3. Delivery with interim updates.

## Fees
- Scoping conversation: no charge.
- Written opinion or position paper: on application.
- Pro advice: from USD 20,001.
- Specialist advice: from USD 50,001.
- Standing advisory mandate: on application.
Figures are minimums; the fee note states the exact amount, confirmed in writing in the client's currency (INR, USD, EUR or GBP) before any charge. Taxes are itemised separately. Indian clients pay by UPI, NEFT or RTGS; overseas clients by PayPal.

## Conflicts and integrity
